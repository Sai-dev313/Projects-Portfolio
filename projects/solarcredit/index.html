About

SolarCredit

Lets renters and apartment dwellers benefit from solar energy they don't generate themselves. Producers log generation and each exported kWh becomes a credit, tradable with neighbors or applied to electricity bills.

What I Built
  • Producer/consumer dashboards and ledger/proof pages reading live from chain events
  • Solidity contract on Sepolia testnet, built with Hardhat
  • Database-first writes with retried on-chain events for resilience
  • Consumer-number validation for Indian electricity providers
  • Row-Level-Security-enforced writes — the browser never writes to tables directly
