A custom, made-to-order furniture business with showrooms in Metpally and Jagtial, Telangana. Needed a site that could showcase handcrafted products and turn visitors into booked showroom visits.

  • Showroom finder with addresses, hours, and map links

  • Mobile-first interactions — swipe carousels, scroll animations
